Recovery after process termination

A worker exits immediately with code 75 at a persisted boundary. A fresh process recovers the state. An independent reader checks the actual active image, its digest and the version floor.

Interruption boundaryRecovered versionInvariants met

v1 is retained before a successful health result is persisted. After that result, recovery completes v2. The campaign uses two artifact sizes (about 315 bytes and 1 MiB). It does not emulate flash-controller behavior or physical power loss.
